Privacy Policy

Webviewer 2.0 is designed with a simple privacy principle: we run no servers, collect no analytics, and send nothing to Webviewer 2.0. All storage stays on your own device or inside your document.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to any Webviewer 2.0 server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry from this add-in.

How your data is stored locally

  • URLs you paste are loaded directly into an iframe inside your PowerPoint slide. They are not sent to any Webviewer 2.0 server.
  • Document settings — the current URL and toolbar visibility are saved in the PowerPoint file's own built-in settings storage so the slide remembers them when you reopen the presentation. This data lives inside your document file.
  • Browser storage (localStorage) — settings such as your toolbar preferences, zoom level, dark mode, and recent URL history are also saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Webviewer 2.0 or any third party.
  • QR codes are generated entirely within your browser using a local library. The URL is never sent to a QR service or any third party.
  • Auto-refresh simply reloads the same iframe at the interval you choose. No requests are made to Webviewer 2.0 servers.

Microsoft Office.js

This add-in uses Microsoft's Office.js library, which is loaded from Microsoft's servers (appsforoffice.microsoft.com). Microsoft may collect telemetry through that library in accordance with Microsoft's Privacy Statement. Webviewer 2.0 has no control over and no access to that data.

Third-party content

The websites you choose to embed are loaded directly from their own servers. Webviewer 2.0 has no control over the content, cookies, or privacy practices of those sites. Refer to each site's own privacy policy for details.

Children's privacy

Webviewer 2.0 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Because we collect no data, any changes will relate only to clarifications, not to new data practices.
