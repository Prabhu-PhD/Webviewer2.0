Privacy Policy

Webviewer 2.0 is designed with a simple privacy principle: your data stays in your document. We collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Webviewer 2.0 does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry of any kind.

How your data is used

  • URLs you paste are loaded directly into an iframe inside your PowerPoint slide. They are not sent to any Webviewer 2.0 server.
  • Document settings — the last URL(s) you loaded are saved in the PowerPoint file's own built-in settings storage so the slide remembers them when you reopen the presentation. This data lives entirely inside your document file and never leaves it.
  • QR codes are generated entirely within your browser using a local library. The URL is never sent to a QR service or any third party.
  • Auto-refresh simply reloads the same iframe at the interval you choose. No requests are made to Webviewer 2.0 servers.

Third-party content

The websites you choose to embed are loaded directly from their own servers. Webviewer 2.0 has no control over the content, cookies, or privacy practices of those sites. Refer to each site's own privacy policy for details.

Children's privacy

Webviewer 2.0 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Because we collect no data, any changes will relate only to clarifications, not to new data practices.
