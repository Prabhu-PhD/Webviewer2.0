Webviewer 2.0

A free PowerPoint add-in that embeds live web content directly on your slides — YouTube, dashboards, timers, Google Slides, and more.

Common reasons a page will not appear

  • The page is not using HTTPS.
  • The site blocks iframe embedding with frame-ancestors or X-Frame-Options headers.
  • You pasted a normal browser URL when the provider has a separate embed or share link.
  • Your network policy prevents Office from reaching the target page.

Tips for best results

  • Use the provider's own embed or share link when available. YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Google Slides, and Google Docs URLs are automatically converted.
  • For split-screen, separate up to 4 URLs with commas in the address bar.
  • Use Auto-Refresh (in the toolbar) to keep live dashboards or feeds current during a presentation.
  • Use Mobile View to see how a site looks at phone width.
  • If a page blocks embedding, use Open in Browser or the QR code button to hand off to a phone or second screen.

Requirements

  • Microsoft 365 or Office 2021 (desktop) with the WebView2 runtime.
  • Content add-ins are a desktop-only feature — not available on iOS or Android Office apps.

Report an issue or request a feature

Open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports are most useful with the URL you were trying to embed and a description of what you expected vs. what happened.
