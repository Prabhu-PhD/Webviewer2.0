Webviewer 2.0

A free PowerPoint add-in that embeds live web pages directly on your slide — dashboards, presentations, forms, maps, whiteboards, and more. Most public pages embed instantly; a few sites block framing (see below). Not a video player; PowerPoint already has that built in.

What works well

  • Dashboards & data — Power BI (publish-to-web), Looker Studio, Tableau Public, Google Sheets
  • Presentations — Google Slides, Canva, Gamma.app
  • Forms & surveys — Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Typeform
  • Whiteboards & collaboration — Miro, Figma, Padlet
  • Interactive & live — Mentimeter, countdown timers, live feeds
  • Maps — Google Maps (use Share → Embed a map), OpenStreetMap
  • Documents — Google Docs, Google Sheets previews, SharePoint
  • Code & demos — CodePen, any public web app or internal tool

URLs for Google Slides, Docs, Sheets, Forms, Miro, Figma, Canva, Airtable, CodePen, Looker Studio, Mentimeter, Padlet, Tableau Public, OpenStreetMap, and Loom are automatically converted to their embed format.

Common reasons a page will not appear

  • The page is not using HTTPS.
  • The site blocks iframe embedding with frame-ancestors or X-Frame-Options headers.
  • You pasted a normal browser URL when the provider has a separate embed or share link.
  • Your network policy prevents Office from reaching the target page.

Tips

  • Separate up to 4 URLs with commas for a side-by-side split-screen view.
  • Use Auto-Refresh to keep live dashboards or feeds current during a presentation.
  • Use Mobile View to preview how a site looks at phone width.
  • If a page blocks embedding, use Open in Browser or the QR code button to hand off to a phone or second screen.

Requirements

  • Microsoft 365 or Office 2021 (desktop) with the WebView2 runtime.
  • Content add-ins are a desktop-only feature — not available on iOS or Android Office apps.

Report an issue or request a feature

Open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports are most useful with the URL you were trying to embed and a description of what you expected vs. what happened.
